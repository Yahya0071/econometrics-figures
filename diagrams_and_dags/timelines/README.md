# Policy Rollout & Institutional Timeline Schematics

This module provides vector-native LaTeX / TikZ schematics for illustrating institutional reforms, staggered policy adoption calendars, and experimental treatment schedules.

---

## 1. Application in Empirical Research

Clear timeline schematics enable readers, referees, and seminar audiences to quickly grasp:
- The timing of pre-treatment baseline measurement.
- The staggered phase-in window across sub-national jurisdictions or cohorts.
- The distinction between early adopters, late adopters, and never-treated comparison units.

---

## 2. Compilation Instructions

Compile the `.tex` file to standalone vector PDF:

```bash
pdflatex policy_rollout_timeline.tex
```
